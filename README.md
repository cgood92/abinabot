# 🤖 Abinabot - Primary Music Companion

A zero-distraction, fully articulated interactive robot companion for LDS Primary Music Leaders.

---

## 🦵 Real Knee Bending & Lengthening Kinematics ([`robot-rig.js`](file:///Users/clintgoodman/dev/abinabot/robot-rig.js))

The lower body movement now uses an inverse kinematic solver where:
1. **The boots stay 100% planted on the ground** at all times.
2. **Knees physically bend and lengthen**:
   - **Squat Down**: The knees bend and flex outward $\rightarrow$ the legs shorten $\rightarrow$ the pelvis, sphere body, head, and arms sink DOWN!
   - **Stand Up Tall**: The knees pull in and straighten $\rightarrow$ the legs lengthen $\rightarrow$ the whole body rises UP tall!
   - **Side-to-Side Sway**: When swaying to the left, the left knee bends while the right leg straightens, shifting the entire body smoothly to the left! When swaying to the right, the right knee bends while the left leg straightens, shifting the body to the right!
3. The corrugated thigh and shin hoses dynamically stretch, compress, and rotate between Hip $\leftrightarrow$ Knee and Knee $\leftrightarrow$ Boot in real time.

## 🗣️ High-Quality Free Voice Options ([`audio.js`](file:///Users/clintgoodman/dev/abinabot/audio.js))

You asked for free alternatives to retro robot synths. The app now provides **three 100% free, natural options**:

### 1. Crystal-Clear Natural Voices (Built into your OS / Browser)
- Accesses all the pleasant, warm, high-definition natural human voices already installed on your Mac / PC (e.g., **Samantha**, **Ava**, **Daniel**, **Karen**, **Google US English**, **Microsoft Natural**).
- No harsh robotic buzzing or monotone—clear, warm, friendly speech that young Primary children can easily understand.
- Includes **Pitch** and **Speed** sliders in the Settings drawer (`⚙️` or press `S`) so you can adjust the warmth or playfulness to your taste.

### 2. Built-in Custom Voice Recorder (Record Abinabot Yourself!)
- Inside the Settings drawer under **Script Prompter & Custom Recording**, you'll see a **"🎙️ Record Line"** button for each line in the song.
- You (or one of your children!) can speak or act out Abinabot's line with your own voice.
- It saves directly in your browser. Whenever that step is reached, Abinabot speaks with your crystal-clear recorded voice clip while animating his mouth and body!

### 3. Ventriloquist / Puppet Mode (Speak Live)
- Turn Abinabot's voice audio **OFF** in settings (or press `M`).
- Abinabot will physically mouth the words, drop his jaw, and perform all gestures on cue while **you speak for him live in the room**!

## 🎮 Keyboard & Clicker Controls

- **`Spacebar` / `Right Arrow (→)` / Presenter Remote (`Page Down`)**: Next line
- **`Left Arrow (←)` / `Backspace`**: Previous line
- **`R`**: Restart song from the beginning
- **`F`**: Fullscreen (fills classroom TV or projector)
- **`V`**: Hands-free Voice Ear toggle (advances on spoken keywords)
- **`M`**: Mute / Unmute Voice
- **`S` or `Escape`**: Open / Close Settings drawer
- **Tap Screen**: Click or tap anywhere on Abinabot to advance!
